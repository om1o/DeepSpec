# DeepSpec Auto Engineer Ad: Visual Evidence + Polar Beta

## Executive Summary

This is the ready ad direction for the next controlled beta push:

**DeepSpec Auto Visual Evidence: AI-assisted scan evidence for engineers and shop teams.**

Use this for engineering-minded mechanics, inspection leads, and small shop owners. The claim is deliberately honest: DeepSpec Auto helps lock onto visible evidence, explain confidence, request more proof, and preserve scan records. It does not claim guaranteed diagnosis, OEM fitment, certified repair guidance, or physical measurement accuracy.

## Primary Ad Copy

Headline:

> AI-assisted scan evidence for engineers and shop teams.

Supporting copy:

> Lock onto the visible target, ground the result in evidence, and ask for the next proof when confidence is not good enough. Paid beta uses Polar sandbox testing before any live payments.

Proof points:

- Target outline
- Confidence range
- Second-angle prompts
- Fail-closed billing

Required disclaimer:

> AI-assisted workflow. Not a guaranteed diagnosis, OEM fitment source, or physical measurement tool.

## Visual Direction

- Apple-like, inspection-first, restrained, high contrast.
- Dark graphite canvas with white type, electric cyan visual-evidence outlines, and green sandbox/proof accents.
- Show the actual product workflow: target outline, confidence range, evidence, next action, and checkout -> webhook -> entitlement -> portal.
- Do not use fake shop names, fake endorsements, fake accuracy claims, or "perfect AI" language.
