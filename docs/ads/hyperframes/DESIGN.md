# DeepSpec Engineer Demo Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

Premium engineering instrument panel for an Apple-like professional app: graphite surfaces, crisp white typography, restrained cyan evidence lines, green sandbox proof states, tight spatial hierarchy, and no decorative blobs or fake cinematic haze.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#05070c`
- Panel: `#10141c`
- Elevated panel: `#171d27`
- Text: `#f8fafc`
- Muted text: `#aeb8c7`
- Visual evidence cyan: `#42d7ff`
- Sandbox proof green: `#6ee7b7`
- Warning amber: `#f6c76b`

## Typography

- Display/UI: `Space Grotesk`
- Data labels: `JetBrains Mono`

## What Not To Do

- Do not claim guaranteed diagnosis, perfect AI, certified repair, or OEM fitment.
- Do not use fake shop names, fake customers, fake endorsements, or unsupported accuracy numbers.
- Do not create fake spatial boxes from vague text evidence.
- Do not use oversized marketing hero cards or decorative orbs.
- Do not turn the payment path into live-payment copy before Polar sandbox gates pass.
