# DeepSpec launch content — drafts

Status: private-beta copy, not posted or paid advertising. A public URL and real trial evidence are still needed. No claimed accuracy percentage, money saved, paying customer or measured time saving.

## Clear promise

**Photograph a car part. Get an AI identification suggestion. Save your inspection.**

DeepSpec helps people investigate unfamiliar car parts, review useful explanations and keep inspection notes together. AI suggestions can be wrong. Confirm part numbers, fitment and functional condition before relying on a result.

## SEO copy to use after the domain is chosen

- Page title: **DeepSpec | Identify Car Parts from Photos**
- Description: **Photograph a car part, explore AI identification suggestions, and save inspection notes with DeepSpec. Review and correct results in one place.**
- Main heading: **A clearer starting point for identifying car parts.**
- First useful guide: **How to photograph a car part for identification** — show an overall view, connector side and readable part number; explain what the photo cannot establish.
- Second guide: **Alternator or A/C compressor? What to inspect** — use reviewed examples with visible differences, genuine source references and explicit uncertainty.

Replace every placeholder `https://deepspec.app` canonical, sitemap and social URL with the chosen production origin. Check that every indexed guide loads without login and its claims match the actual product. Authenticated scans and personal inspection records are not marketing pages. Preview environments should not be promoted as the permanent site.

## Short demo script

0–4 seconds: show the part and ask “What would you check first?”

4–12 seconds: photograph it and show the real identification suggestion. If a wait is shortened, label the cut.

12–20 seconds: show one useful explanation and the part-number/fitment checks still needed.

20–28 seconds: add an inspection note, save, reopen and show the note survived.

28–30 seconds: “DeepSpec private beta. Help us test it.”
