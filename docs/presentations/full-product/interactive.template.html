DeepSpec / the full product story

See the part.
Keep the evidence.

Photo-assisted identification and human inspection records for the people who handle car parts.

Photo → identify → understand → report or correct

A working prototype targeting a real-user V1 within about one month.
Illustrative artwork. No customer results are claimed.

01 / Purpose

A part is useful.
A part with a clear record is easier to work with.

IDENTITY

What is this part?

Photos, labels and reference information need to be brought together before an identity is trusted.

CONDITION

What was checked?

A part can look intact while its function is still unknown. Appearance and actual tests need separate records.

HANDOFF

Where are the notes?

The next person needs the photo, the conclusion and the checks behind it, without repeating the whole process.

These are the workflow problems DeepSpec is designed to address. Their frequency and cost need to be measured with users.

04 / Capture

A useful answer starts with a useful photo.

Camera or upload

Work with a new image or one already on the device.

Guidance and focus

Quality checks, framing and part isolation help the user focus the scan on the intended object.

Retake when needed

A better angle or clearer label can add evidence. An uncertain result should remain uncertain.

Cutout paths depend on the device and can fall back to a crop or full image. This is not a 3D scan or calibrated measurement system.

05 / AI assistance

A starting point for investigation.
With the reasoning kept nearby.

SUGGESTION

Possible identity

Read the candidate part, its purpose and the clues visible in the image.

EVIDENCE

What still needs checking

Review uncertainty, labels and available references. Confirm exact part number and fitment against a trusted source.

FOLLOW-UP

Ask about the scan

Continue a conversation attached to that scan, so the photo and its context stay together.

AI confidence is not proof of correct identity, compatibility, function or safety. Follow-up chat requires an available AI provider.

06 / Inspection

Three different questions.
Three kinds of evidence.

Inspection evidence and its limits
QuestionWhat the person recordsWhat it establishes
Which part is it?Part name, number and identity evidence.A self-reported identity check.
What can I see?Visible condition and notes.Observed appearance, not internal function.
Does it work?Test method, result and inspector name.The recorded test outcome, not certification.

Eight fields. Defaults: Not inspected and Not tested. Human evidence remains separate from the AI answer.

16 / Deliberate data collection

A correction starts a review.
It does not automatically train a model.

  1. Report the problem.
    Wrong identity, vehicle, condition, value, explanation or AR placement; keep the original result.
  2. Review the evidence.
    New → reviewed → confirmed issue → fixed. Separate user corrections from verified labels.
  3. Check permission and quality.
    Explicit consent, privacy review, opt-out and deletion before approving an example.
  4. Evaluate a versioned dataset.
    Record origin, label, model version and membership. Improve prompts first; train only when justified.

Target architecture. Rating/corrections and model metadata exist; durable training consent, internal review and dataset lineage still need implementation. No automatic retraining is claimed.
