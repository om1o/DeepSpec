# Dated component comparisons

Owners can connect an exact original from an earlier saved scan to an exact original from a follow-up scan, record a human observation, and revisit that evidence in the mechanic report. Guided repeat capture carries the earlier reference and reserved follow-up/comparison IDs in the durable photo draft. The earlier image is a framing reference, not another new image sent for identification.

## Evidence and limits

- Photo-added and scan-saved times remain distinct from the optional, user-reported observation dates. Imported photographs do not acquire a claimed exposure date.
- Confirming the same vehicle and component is an explicit human statement with a basis. Matching labels, vehicle metadata, or timestamps do not establish identity.
- Original raster bytes are SHA-256 fingerprinted, with dimensions, photo ID, saved vehicle context and analysis reference. Signed URL renewal does not change this identity. Missing, changed and unverifiable evidence remain distinct; no alternative photo is substituted.
- Rotation, proportional scaling and translation affect the display only. Originals remain available. Alignment is not calibration, a thickness measurement or proof that two views are comparable.
- Human observations distinguish apparent change, no obvious change, inconclusive and not reviewed. Viewpoint, lighting and surface/cleaning notes remain attached. No rust-growth rate, remaining material, cause, repair success or safe-to-drive conclusion is generated by this workflow.
- Comparisons and correction alternatives are immutable records separate from AI results and inspection status. Source deletion does not intentionally erase comparison history. User account deletion removes its cloud history.
- Device append/import and unfinished-draft replacement use owner-scoped IndexedDB transactions. Cloud reads and synchronization are explicit. New cloud comparisons require both source scans already owned and saved; existing immutable records can still be read after a source disappears.
- The report follows comparison references, embeds available originals, verifies each historical reference against its embedded source, and checks local map/history/comparison revisions in one final transaction. Historical notes remain visible when the source is missing or changed.

## Primary-source design inputs

Reviewed 5 November 2026. These are documentation patterns adapted for cars, not validated automotive diagnostic methods or copied procedures/assets.

- [NPS, Repeat Photography](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/aps-20-1-6.htm), updated June 2021: stable viewpoints and landmarks, repeatability and proportional display alignment. No example photographs or agency branding copied.
- [FHWA, Photographing for Documentation Purposes](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/publications/research/infrastructure/structures/ltbp/16007/027.cfm), January 2016, explicitly archived: retain overview/detail context, photo identity, dates and comments. Its archived operational requirements are not asserted as current automotive requirements.
- [FHWA, Inspection Procedures FAQ Q313-1/2](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/bridge/nbis2022/qanda/08.cfm), entries dated March 2023: photographs supplement inspection and do not supply tactile, auditory or load-response evidence.
- [Supabase RLS](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/database/postgres/row-level-security) and [JavaScript insert](https://supabase.com/docs/reference/javascript/insert): owner predicates, explicit grants, insert-only history and response verification. The changelog and relevant CLI-v3/TLS notices were checked; the migration file was created by the installed v3 CLI. No hosted schema or credentials were changed.

## Acceptance requirements

Check secondary-photo selection, identity reset after changing either endpoint, truthful unknown/import dates, contradictory vehicle context, lighting/angle warnings, original-byte preservation, account isolation, stale-draft rejection, interrupted capture completion without another provider request, immutable correction forks, missing/changed originals, and self-contained report output.

Source tests, disposable SQL and synthetic local-browser checks do not establish real-vehicle accuracy, hosted cloud acceptance or physical iPad/Safari reliability. Those remain separate release requirements. Automatic deployment is disabled for this branch; the migration is unapplied.
