# DeepSpec tester interview and social plan

## Ten-minute tester interview

Ask the tester to use DeepSpec before explaining it. Observe silently until they stop or become blocked.

1. What did you think DeepSpec would do before you opened it?
2. Show me how you would identify this part without DeepSpec.
3. What did you expect to happen after taking the photo?
4. Which part of the answer did you trust, and why?
5. Which part made you uncertain or confused?
6. Was the highlighted object the object you intended to scan?
7. What follow-up question did you naturally want to ask?
8. Would you reopen or share this record later? For what job?
9. What would make you stop using DeepSpec?
10. Who else has this problem often enough to care?

Record the task outcome, wrong/right/not-sure judgment, time to result, save/reopen result, and one verbatim phrase. Do not ask whether they “like” the app until after the task; that produces polite answers instead of evidence.

## First X posts

Use the existing `public/brand/deepspec-social-card.png` or an uncropped product screenshot. Do not use a fake model result or promise accuracy that has not been measured.

**Build-in-public post**

> We are testing DeepSpec with family before promoting it widely. Photograph a vehicle part, review the visible evidence, ask a follow-up, and keep the result with your correction. The hard part is not producing an answer. It is making uncertainty and mistakes easy to see.

**Problem post**

> A generic AI answer disappears into a chat. DeepSpec keeps the photo, probable part, visible evidence, sources, follow-up, and human correction in one record. We are testing whether that record is actually useful for DIY owners, trainees, and parts sellers.

**Feedback post**

> DeepSpec V1 is not a repair certificate or exact-fitment guarantee. We are looking for the cases where it picks the wrong object, gives the wrong part, or explains too little. Those failures are the product work.

## Publishing rule

Post screenshots only after removing names, plates, addresses, account details, and private scan identifiers. Get the tester's permission before sharing their image or quote. Codex can prepare images and copy in this repository, but this workspace does not have an X account connector, so a human must approve and publish each post.
