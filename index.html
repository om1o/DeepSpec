DeepSpec: AI-assisted vehicle part identification

DeepSpec helps car owners, technicians, and parts sellers photograph an unfamiliar part, inspect the visible evidence, ask follow-up questions, and keep a saved record.

A DeepSpec result includes a probable part name, confidence, visible observations, concerns, next-photo guidance, and source links. The record keeps the original image with later ratings, corrections, notes, and focused follow-up questions.

The app supports practical learning and documentation. It does not treat one photo as proof of exact fitment, hidden condition, functional testing, value, or repair safety. High-risk systems still need qualified inspection and vehicle-specific evidence.
