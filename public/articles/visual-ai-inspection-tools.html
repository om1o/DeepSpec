Deep Spec guide

Visual AI inspection tools for cars and technical work

Visual AI inspection tools can save time when they organize what is visible, capture evidence, and help people ask the next better question. They become dangerous when they guess with too much confidence.

Try Deep Spec Scan a car part and save the result as structured evidence.

Can AI tools save time for engineers?

Yes, when they reduce repetitive lookup work. Engineers and technical builders often move between photos, labels, manuals, notes, and search results. AI can create a faster first pass by summarizing visible evidence and preparing the next lookup.

Why Deep Spec is narrow on purpose

Deep Spec is not trying to answer every car repair question. The product is narrow: camera-first car part identification, visible concern detection, safety triage, saved scans, and follow-up explanations.

What makes the data useful later

The useful dataset is not random car photos. It is structured scan records:

  • Compressed photo
  • Model answer
  • Scan category
  • Safety triage
  • Rating and correction
  • Notes and follow-up chat
  • Timestamp

That data can later support evaluation, model comparison, and safer fine-tuning.
