# Alternator or A/C compressor? What to inspect

Both parts may be metal, belt-driven accessories mounted low in an engine bay. The pulley alone is not enough. Look for the type of connection, housing design, printed label, and location.

## Alternator clues

- A heavy electrical output terminal plus a smaller control connector.
- A vented aluminum housing where copper windings may be visible.
- No two large refrigerant pipe connections entering the case.
- An electrical-rating or alternator part-number label.

## A/C compressor clues

- Two refrigerant line connections or a manifold block on the body.
- A cylindrical or compact pump-like case with mounting ears.
- On some designs, a clutch plate at the pulley face. Variable compressors may not have an obvious external clutch.
- A refrigerant or compressor label. Never loosen a line to inspect it.

Appearance does not prove exact fitment, internal function, refrigerant charge, electrical output, or whether a part is safe to reuse. Verify replacement decisions with the VIN, part number, service information, and appropriate testing.
