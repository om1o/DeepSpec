# How to photograph a car part for identification

A useful identification photo preserves the clues that separate similar parts: where the item is installed, what connects to it, how it is mounted, and what text is printed or stamped on it.

## Take these three photos

1. **Overall view:** Show the complete part and enough of the engine bay, wheel area, or underside to understand its location.
2. **Connector side:** Show electrical plugs, hose fittings, mounting ears, ports, pulleys, or belt routing.
3. **Readable label:** Capture the part number, manufacturer name, casting mark, QR code, or warning label. Tap to focus and verify that the text is readable.

Wipe the lens, use steady indirect light, keep the intended part near the center, and retake blurred labels. Add the vehicle year, make, model, engine, and installation area. A photo may support a probable part family, but exact fitment often requires a VIN, readable number, service information, or physical measurement.

Do not reach near a running engine, hot exhaust, moving belt, pressurized line, raised vehicle, or damaged high-voltage component just to improve a photo.
