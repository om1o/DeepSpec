# Car damage AI scanner: how to use AI without over-trusting it

A car damage AI scanner can help spot visible clues like rust, leaks, cracks, missing bolts, frayed wiring, melted plastic, or fluid residue. It should not replace a mechanic for safety-critical problems.

Try Deep Spec: https://deepspec.app/

## What AI can help with

AI can help a car owner describe what is visible in a photo. That matters because most people do not know the names of car parts, and searching for "thing leaking under hood" wastes time.

A helpful scanner should turn a photo into plain language:

- probable part category
- visible clues
- possible concerns
- whether the photo needs a better angle
- what to do next

## What AI should not claim

Deep Spec should not certify repairs, guarantee fitment, name exact OEM part numbers, or rank repair shops as "best" without real data. A trustworthy AI helper admits limits.

## Damage that should escalate

Brakes, steering, suspension, fuel, airbags, electrical burning, severe leaks, and unclear high-risk damage should be checked before driving or repairing. Deep Spec can explain the visible clue, but it should not walk a user through risky repairs.

## Why saved feedback matters

Every scan can become better training and evaluation data when it includes the photo, model answer, category, user rating, correction, notes, and outcome. That is how Deep Spec can improve beyond a generic AI answer.
