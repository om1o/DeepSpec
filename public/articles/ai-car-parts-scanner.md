# AI car parts scanner: what it should do before you trust it

An AI car parts scanner should help a normal car owner understand what they are looking at without pretending to be a certified mechanic.

Deep Spec is built around that idea: open the scanner, point the rear camera at a car part, hold steady, capture a clear frame, and get a cautious explanation. The goal is simple: identify car parts with camera evidence first, then use AI as a careful helper.

Try Deep Spec: https://deepspec.app/

## What a useful scanner explains

A good car part AI helper should answer a few practical questions:

- What is the probable part?
- How confident is the result?
- What does the part do?
- What visible concerns are in the photo?
- Is this safe to keep inspecting, or should a mechanic verify it?

That is different from a parts catalog. Deep Spec does not promise exact OEM part numbers, fitment, live pricing, or repair certification in v1.

## Why camera quality matters

AI car part finding gets worse when photos are blurry, dark, too close, or missing context. That is why Deep Spec uses a smart reticle and stillness check before capture. The scanner is designed to collect a better photo before asking AI to identify anything.

## What happens after a scan

Deep Spec saves the scan, AI result, category, rating, correction, notes, and follow-up chat on the device. That creates a useful dataset over time: not just guesses, but real scans with user feedback.

## Safety boundary

For brakes, steering, suspension, fuel, airbags, electrical burning, severe leaks, or unclear high-risk damage, Deep Spec should tell the user to verify with a mechanic.
