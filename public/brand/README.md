# DeepSpec brand assets

The primary DeepSpec identity is the flat white inverse logo. Use it for the app header, authentication screens, saved scans, reports, social previews, browser icons, and installed app icons.

- `deepspec-logo.webp`: primary white logo and wordmark, optimized for app screens.
- `deepspec-logo.png`: primary white logo and wordmark for social metadata and other PNG consumers.
- `../icon-192.png` and `../icon-512.png`: primary white symbol on navy for browser and installed-app icons.
- `deepspec-blue-glass-intro.webp`: optional blue-glass accent for intro artwork only. It does not replace the white primary identity.

Logo artwork stays static. Do not rotate, pulse, shimmer, morph, or animate the mark or wordmark. Intro motion may move surrounding photography, camera framing, or interface elements while the artwork itself stays unchanged.

Use the supplied artwork without changing its geometry, wordmark, proportions, or colors. The navy background ensures contrast for the white identity.
